# Closing & Action Plan (4:03–4:15)

Participants write one concrete 30-day commitment before they leave; the closing sends them out with that commitment, not more content.

### Closing script (4:13–4:15)

"Four hours ago most of you had never built an AI agent. Now every one of you has a tutor for your own course, and a set of materials you can use next week.

Three things to take with you.

First, the rule: AI should guide students, not do the work for them. Every time you change your tutor, run the five tests again.

Second, detection starts a conversation; design prevents the problem. Before you worry about a Turnitin score, ask whether your assignment could be done by AI alone, and change it if it could.

Third, start small. One course, one section, one topic. Read what students actually ask, and improve it. Your tutor in week six will be far better than in week one.

You're not doing this alone. \[Name the follow-up channel.\] Share what works and what breaks. In 30 days I'll check in on the plan you just wrote.

Thank you to IBA-CICT and SMCS, to our table helpers, and to all of you. Please fill in the feedback form on screen before you go."
