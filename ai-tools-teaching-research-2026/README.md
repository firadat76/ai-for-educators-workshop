# AI Tools for Teaching and Research: 4-hour SMCS faculty workshop

IBA-CICT and SMCS · October 2026 · Dr. Faisal Iradat

The workshop runs in four sessions plus an action plan:

1. AI tools at top universities
2. A Copilot Studio tutor or research coach, embedded in Sakai
3. EduGears AI Studio as an LTI 1.3 tool in Sakai
4. Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM): your paper as a podcast
5. Action plan: redesign one assessment
