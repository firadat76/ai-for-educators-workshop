The research difference

It answers from your sources, and shows where

A chatbot from memory

  • Answers from its training data
  • Can invent references that look real
  • You can’t see where a claim came from

Gemini Notebook

  • Built to answer from the sources you upload
  • Clickable citations jump to the quoted passage
  • You choose exactly which sources it reads

Still check: a citation can point to the right page and still misread it.

Source: Google Gemini Notebook Help, “Use citations” (checked Oct 2026)

2:49. Connect back to Session 2’s reference test. A general chatbot answers from memory and can invent references. Gemini Notebook is built to answer from the sources you give it, and its answers carry inline citations you can click to jump to the quoted passage. That makes it far better for research and for students working from readings. But grounded does not mean correct: it can still misread a table or overstate a finding.
