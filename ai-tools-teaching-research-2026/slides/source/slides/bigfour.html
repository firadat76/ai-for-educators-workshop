Four assistants account for almost every institutional deal. ChatGPT Edu: Oxford became the first UK university to give it to all staff and students in September 2025; NUS from 31 August 2026; the California State University system to more than 460,000 students. Columbia and Penn offer it to faculty and staff for a fee, and Harvard’s Faculty of Arts and Sciences now provides it only on request. Copilot Chat is the most common default because it comes with Microsoft 365; Manchester went furthest with full Microsoft 365 Copilot for about 65,000 staff and students. Gemini and Gemini Notebook, which Google renamed from NotebookLM in July 2026, come through Google Workspace; Stanford runs Gemini Enterprise in a pilot to August 2027, and NTU gives every undergraduate Gemini Enterprise from August 2026. Claude: Dartmouth gave it to all students, faculty and staff in December 2025, LSE students can request it, Harvard FAS offers it on request, Stanford includes it in its pilot, Princeton lists it for faculty, and Penn licenses it per user.
