IIT Madras is notable for research rather than licensing. In August 2025 it became OpenAI’s research partner, backed by $500,000 for long-term studies of how AI affects learning outcomes. Its BS in Data Science piloted AI tools with Scaler with 500 students in early 2025, including AI mock interviews and round-the-clock coding support. We found no campus-wide licence for ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot or Claude. Across India, OpenAI announced in February 2026 that ChatGPT Edu would reach more than 100,000 students, faculty and staff across IIT Delhi, IIM Ahmedabad, AIIMS New Delhi, Manipal, UPES and Pearl Academy. AICTE announced more than 100,000 free ChatGPT Go licences, valid for six months, for its students and faculty in 2025. Policy lags: in a University of Delhi study of Library and Information Science students, nearly 90% said their institutions lacked formal AI-use policies.
