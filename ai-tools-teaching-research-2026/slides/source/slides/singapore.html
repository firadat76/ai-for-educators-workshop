Singapore is moving fastest. NUS gives ChatGPT Edu and Codex to all students, faculty and staff from 31 August 2026, alongside its own AI-Know platform, and from the August 2026 intake makes AI courses compulsory for all undergraduates, including the first-year module Applied Generative AI. NTU gives every undergraduate Gemini Enterprise, Google AI Studio and Vertex AI from August 2026, and its NALA platform lets educators build AI tutors on their own course materials. Its goal is AI in 40% of courses by 2030, up from about 5%. SMU gives every student free AI tools such as Gemini Education Plus, and adds new AI courses in computing, law and accountancy in 2026/27. Students are heavy users: in an OpenAI-commissioned survey of 514 students in August 2026, 94% used AI several times a week or more and 35% had built their own AI agent or assistant.
