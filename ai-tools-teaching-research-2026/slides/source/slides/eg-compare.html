EduGears AI Studio · worked example

Faculty who attended the Copilot Studio session will ask how this differs. Copilot Studio builds a conversational agent: very flexible, but it does not produce lessons, quizzes or grades. EduGears produces a structured course with assessment and gradebook passback. A good pattern: build the course in EduGears and keep a Copilot Studio tutor beside it in Sakai.
