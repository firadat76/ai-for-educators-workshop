Across continental Europe, Microsoft Copilot Chat is the default because universities already pay for Microsoft 365, with Gemini and Gemini Notebook second through Google Workspace. Privacy shapes everything else. Amsterdam built UvA AI Chat in-house, and Utrecht’s UU AI-Chat is built on it. VU Amsterdam does not license ChatGPT, because there is no data-processing agreement, so teachers may not require it. Helsinki offers CurreChat for teaching, running OpenAI models on the university’s Azure. ETH Zurich, EPFL and CSCS co-developed Apertus, an open Swiss language model. France’s Mistral AI alliance had 35 public institutions by mid-2025 and about 81 by early 2026, and the higher-education ministry started a pilot of a Mistral assistant, including a virtual tutor, at 21 institutions from September 2025.
