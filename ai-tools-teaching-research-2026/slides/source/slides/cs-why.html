AI for Educators: Future-Ready Teaching · IBA-CICT and SMCS

“Here is the reality we all know. Our students are already using AI. Some use it well. Many use it to skip the thinking we are trying to teach. We can’t stop that by banning it, and we shouldn’t hand our teaching over to it either. So today’s question is simple: how do we make AI work for learning, instead of against it?”
