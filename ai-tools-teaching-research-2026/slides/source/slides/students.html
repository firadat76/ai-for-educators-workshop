10

ChatGPT is the most-used tool in every survey that names tools. The Digital Education Council’s 2024 global survey (3,839 students, 16 countries) found 66% use ChatGPT, with Grammarly and Microsoft Copilot at 25% each. A 2025 German study by Hochschule Darmstadt (4,910 students at 395 institutions) found about 82% use the free ChatGPT and just under 45% use DeepL; it is a self-selected sample. HEPI’s 2026 survey of 1,054 UK undergraduates finds 95% use AI in at least one way. The caution: Harvard’s Faculty of Arts and Sciences wound down its ChatGPT Edu pilot after June 2026, citing cost and undergraduate uptake that was “far less than we anticipated”; continued use now needs administrative approval. Licences and real usage are not the same thing.
