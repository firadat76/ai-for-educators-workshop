The short answer

Top universities run on general-purpose AI assistants, licensed securely: ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini with Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM), and Claude.

This is the headline. The tools professors and students use at top universities are the big general-purpose assistants, usually provided through a university licence or the university’s own secure portal. MagicSchool and Brisk are good products, but they mainly target school teachers: Stanford SCALE calls MagicSchool “one of the largest K-12 AI platforms,” and Brisk is described as an AI teaching assistant for K-12 educators. They do not show up in any of the university tool lists reviewed for this talk.
