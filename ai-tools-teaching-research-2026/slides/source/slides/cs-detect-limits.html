Limitations to say out loud

What the AI score can’t tell you

*%

Scores of 1–19% show no number. Treat them as no signal.

300

Words of prose minimum. Code, bullets and tables aren’t read.

3

Languages: English, Spanish, Japanese. Not Urdu.

0%

Is not proof of honest work. Edited AI text can slip through.

False positives happen. Formal, concise writing and second-language writers can be flagged. Many of our students write in their second language.

Source: Turnitin guides; UW-Whitewater faculty guidance, Jan 2026

Turnitin’s “under 1%” false-positive figure applies to documents with 20% or more AI-flagged text, not to every paper; below 20% false positives are more likely, which is why those scores show as an asterisk. The detector reads 300 to 30,000 words of long-form prose in .docx, .pdf, .txt or .rtf. Grammar tools and AI use you permitted can also raise the score. The score covers only qualifying prose, so it may not reflect the whole document.
