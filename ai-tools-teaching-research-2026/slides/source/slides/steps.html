Hands-on · 25 minutes

Turn your paper into a podcast

  1. Open notebooklm.google, sign in with Google, create a notebook
  2. Add a source: your published paper (PDF) or a course reading
  3. Studio → Audio Overview → Customize: Critique or Debate → Generate
  4. While it generates: open Mind Map, then ask 3 questions and click each citation
  5. Play 2 minutes at your table, then generate a Quiz

Ask it, for example

What is the main claim, and which evidence supports it?

What would a sceptical reviewer attack first?

Explain the method to a second-year student.

Upload only published papers or open readings. No unpublished research or student data on a personal account.

2:50 to 3:15. Step 3 first, because audio takes a few minutes to generate. Critique is the most fun with your own paper: two hosts give it constructive feedback. Debate works well for a contested reading. While it generates, build the mind map and ask three questions, clicking each numbered citation to check it lands on the right passage. At 3:05 each table plays two minutes of one podcast. Then everyone generates a quiz. If Wi-Fi is slow, play the pre-made podcast from the trainer laptop.
