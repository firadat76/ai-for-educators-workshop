Around the big assistants sits a layer of specialist tools. For writing and language, Grammarly (25% of students in the 2024 DEC survey) and DeepL (just under 45% of German students in 2025) are the most-used. For research, Oxford’s Bodleian Library teaches Elicit and ResearchRabbit, Yale Library is trialling Consensus until December 2026, and ETH lists Scopus AI, Scite and Perplexity. In coding, about 20% of Harvard undergraduate AI users used GitHub Copilot in a 2024 survey, NUS Computing is introducing Codex, and Princeton lists Claude Code for faculty. The most interesting category for teaching is course tutors: AI bots built by teachers on their own course materials, such as NTU’s NALA, VU Amsterdam’s Cogniti, Utrecht’s custom bots and Harvard’s CS50 Duck.
