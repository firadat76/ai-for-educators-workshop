Sources: Harvard Gazette (Sep 2026); NTU NALA project; Univ. of Sydney (Jun 2026); UNC OASIS

2 minutes, right after the CSE 455 demo. What you just saw is what top universities do. Harvard’s CS50 Duck, since 2023, is told in plain English not to give outright answers, and the team’s own code evaluates each reply before the student sees it, sometimes rejecting or retrying it. NTU’s NALA is a no-code builder: educators upload module PDFs, set starting questions and read a dashboard of conversations; it has been piloted in nine NTU schools since 2023. The University of Sydney’s Cogniti lets teachers build agents on their own materials; hundreds of Sydney educators use it, and Leiden University uses it with about 2,000 students in four faculties. And UNC Chapel Hill tested a Copilot Studio course assistant in three large courses in Fall 2025: the same tool we use today.
