Germany is different because of data-protection rules. Instead of each university signing its own vendor deal, states and consortia run shared platforms. KI:connect.nrw gives all 36 public universities in North Rhine-Westphalia anonymised access to ChatGPT and other models, covering about 607,000 students. HAWKI, an open-source interface first built at HAWK Hildesheim, was used at around 30 universities in 2024; Baden-Württemberg’s bwGPT runs on it at 15. GWDG’s Chat AI through the Academic Cloud offers several models free, including open-weight ones, and Schleswig-Holstein adopted it for all nine state higher-education institutions in July 2026. Students still use the free ChatGPT heavily (about 82% in the 2025 Darmstadt study) and DeepL (just under 45%).
