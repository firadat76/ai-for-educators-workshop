A paper-reading lesson: rewrite recall into reasoning

The AI’s first draft (recall)Your rewrite (reasoning)
What method does the paper use?Why did the authors choose this method over a simpler one, and what does it cost them?
What did the study find?Which finding would change if the sample were Pakistani firms, and why?
List the paper’s limitations.Which limitation most weakens the main claim? Defend your choice.

Generate 5–8 questions for the lesson, rewrite each this way, then attach the practice set at module level: Modules → Add practice.

For anyone who supervises research or teaches with papers. Make one lesson about a paper students must read, as the CSE 455 pilot does with one paper per week. EduGears’ “Hard” questions came out as recall in our build. The fix is to rewrite each one to ask why or how. Practice sets can’t attach to a lesson, only to a module, using Add practice on the module card. Check with Preview as Student.
