1 minute. These are the lessons peers learned the hard way, and each one is already in today’s design. One: in the Harvard Gazette interview (September 2026), the interviewer noted the Duck still answered with blocks of code up to a quarter of the time despite its instructions; David Malan put this down to randomness and training data. That is why we test, not trust. Two: grounding in course files. Three: reading what students actually ask, which is week 3 of your 30-day plan. Four: once a tutor works, role-play agents are the next step; Leiden pharmacy students practise a challenging interaction with an older customer, and Auckland University of Technology has a sports-marketing mentor called Jack.
