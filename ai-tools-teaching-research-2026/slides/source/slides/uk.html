UK universities tend to pick one flagship. Oxford was the first UK university to give ChatGPT Edu to everyone, after a year-long pilot with about 750 academics, research staff, postgraduate research students and professional services staff. Manchester went the Microsoft route with full Microsoft 365 Copilot plus training for about 65,000 staff and students, with rollout due to finish by summer 2026. LSE gives every student free access to Claude (on request) and Copilot Chat. Imperial’s dAIsy and Edinburgh’s ELM are the universities’ own secure gateways to several models. HEPI’s 2026 survey shows provision is rising fast: 38% of students say their institution provides AI tools, up from 23% in 2025 and 9% in 2024. ChatGPT is named most often, followed by Copilot.
