For your course outline

One line per assessment: red, amber or green

RED

No AI. In-class quizzes, mid-term and final exams.

AMBER

AI for support only. Brainstorm, explain, revise from readings. Not to write, solve or analyse. Say which tool and what for.

GREEN

AI encouraged. Tasks where using and critiquing AI output is the point.

Gemini Notebook example: green for revising from readings you have done; red for submitting its summaries as your own work.

3:35. Each person writes one line per assessment in their course. The labels are spelled out, so the slide works in black and white too. The Gemini Notebook line in the handout is a ready example: “You may use Gemini Notebook to revise from course readings. Its summaries do not replace reading, and any AI-generated text you submit must be declared.” The HEC draft policy expects universities to ask students to disclose AI use, and this is how you make that concrete.
