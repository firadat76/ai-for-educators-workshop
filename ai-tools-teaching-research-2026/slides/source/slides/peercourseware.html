2 minutes, before the EduGears demo. Generating courseware with AI is already normal at peer universities; the difference is that it lives inside the LMS and the teacher signs off every item. Blackboard’s AI Design Assistant suggests course modules and drafts test questions and rubrics inside the course; Blackboard quotes staff at Leeds and Wichita State who use it. The University of Dundee’s rules are a good model: staff must confirm, edit and accept or reject every item, tell students when AI made content, and Dundee has not enabled whole-course or rubric generation. Georgia Tech’s Jill Watson, an AI teaching assistant, plugs into Canvas or Blackboard as an LTI tool. That is exactly the LTI route we are asking CICT to open for EduGears in Sakai.
