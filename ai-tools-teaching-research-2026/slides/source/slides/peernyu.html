Source: NYU Teaching and Learning with Generative AI Symposium, NotebookLM sessions (Oct 2025)

2 minutes. NYU devoted its 2025 teaching symposium to NotebookLM, so we have concrete examples from faculty. A graduate music theory seminar used a notebook each week for long readings, and every student had to ask at least one specific question. An intro public policy course had students start from about 15 sources, add public data, build timelines and literature reviews, and check their drafts against the sources. An intro programming lecture of 100-plus students used it with student feedback data to design in-class formative questions. A Master’s course in international cultural relations compared four national cultural policies in four languages. And one caution: a study at NYU Shanghai found students’ reading shifted from interpreting texts toward confirming what they already expected. The fix is the same as today’s rule: design questions that make students think.
