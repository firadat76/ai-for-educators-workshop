The worked example: CSE 455 Computer Security

The outline

  • 14 weeks, two 75-minute sessions each
  • A lab and a research paper every week
  • Exams are supervised and count 54%
  • AI use is set per assessment, levels 1–3

Studio allows 6 modules at most, so we grouped the 14 weeks by theme.

ModuleWeeks
1 · Foundations, threat modelling, the lab1–2

EduGears AI Studio · worked example

Our example is a final-year course with a demanding design: a weekly lab, a weekly 2025–26 research paper and supervised descriptive exams. The New Course form caps modules at six, which forced a design decision early: group weeks by theme. We filled Module 2, Web Security, as the pilot because it is self-contained and uses free web labs.
