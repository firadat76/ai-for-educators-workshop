Recommendations based on the findings in this deck

22

What should SMCS take from this? One: focus training on the core stack that peer universities actually provide: ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini with NotebookLM, and Claude. Two: add one research tool such as Elicit or Consensus; Oxford’s Bodleian Library teaches Elicit, and Yale Library is running a year-long trial of Consensus. Three: build your own course bots; you don’t need a platform like NALA, because Custom GPTs, Gemini Gems and Claude Projects let any lecturer ground a tutor in their own materials today. Four: invest in assessment redesign rather than AI detection, following top universities such as Yale, Vanderbilt and Johns Hopkins that switched Turnitin’s AI detector off. MagicSchool and Brisk are fine for lesson ideas, but they are built mainly for K-12 and not what peer universities use.
