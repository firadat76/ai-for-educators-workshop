Three principles

Every tutor we build today follows these

Socratic by design

The tutor asks before it tells.

Integrity by design

It never completes graded work.

You stay the teacher

It knows only what you give it, and says so when it doesn’t know.

AI for Educators: Future-Ready Teaching · IBA-CICT and SMCS

Socratic by design: the tutor asks a question before it explains. Integrity by design: it refuses to write, solve or check graded work. You stay the teacher: it only knows the material you upload, and it should admit uncertainty and send students back to you. These three ideas show up as specific lines in every instruction template.
