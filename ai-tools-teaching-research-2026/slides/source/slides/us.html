No two Ivies made the same choice. Copilot Chat is the common free baseline because it comes with Microsoft 365. Yale and Penn built their own multi-model portals, Clarity and PennChat, so people can switch between OpenAI, Anthropic and Google models securely. Dartmouth announced Claude for all students, faculty and staff in December 2025, and uses Amazon Bedrock to build custom apps for campus operations. Harvard’s Faculty of Arts and Sciences gives everyone Gemini and offers Claude and ChatGPT Edu through a licence request form; after June 2026, ChatGPT Edu needs administrative approval. Stanford is running all three big vendors in a free pilot to August 2027: Gemini switches on automatically, while ChatGPT Edu and Claude need a short request form. Columbia charges $300 per user a year for ChatGPT Edu.
