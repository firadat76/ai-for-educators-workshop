Student access is personal

  • No campus-wide licences found at the six universities checked

Sources: ProPakistani (Aug 2026); Dawn (Oct 2025); Express Tribune (Aug 2026)

Nationally, two things matter. First, HEC shared a draft policy on AI in higher education in August 2026. Each university must write its own policy, set up an AI committee and report to HEC annually. Students must disclose AI use, and every course outline must say what is allowed. Importantly, students cannot be penalised on an AI-detection report alone, which sits uneasily with policies that lean on Turnitin’s AI score. Universities must also integrate AI literacy within two years and run annual training for faculty, students and staff. Second, student access is personal rather than institutional: in October 2025 Google made its AI Pro plan, including Gemini and NotebookLM, free for a year for Pakistani students aged 18 and over, and the August 2026 Pakistan–Google MoU promises students a free year of Google AI Plus and Gemini. In practice, many of our students can already get premium Google tools if they claim them.
