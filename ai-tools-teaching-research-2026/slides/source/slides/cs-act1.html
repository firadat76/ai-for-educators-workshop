Copilot Studio · steps from the trainer’s own build

Go to copilotstudio.microsoft.com and sign in with the IBA account. Name format: “[Course code] Study Coach” or “[Course code] Research Coach”. Call each checkpoint aloud; green card = done, red card = stuck, helpers go to red cards first. Fast finishers: swap laptops and try to break a neighbour’s agent. Watch for anyone uploading student data or exam papers. The most common failure is an agent that gives full answers or lists references; the fix is moving the refusal or never-invent rule to the top and adding the example exchange. Publishing: Settings › Security › Authentication › No authentication › Save, then Publish, then Channels › Demo website. Menu names can differ slightly between Copilot Studio releases.
