Design beats detection

Make AI alone not enough

  • Local and current: a Karachi firm, this month’s SBP decision
  • Personal: the student’s own decision, with reasons
  • Process graded: proposal, draft, final, reflection
  • Live: a 5-minute viva or in-class defence
  • Class-specific: the case or dataset from your class

Say it in your course outline

Red · no AI: quizzes, mid-term, final

Amber · support only: brainstorm, explain, grammar; disclose it

Green · encouraged: tasks where critiquing AI is the point

AI for Educators: Future-Ready Teaching · IBA-CICT and SMCS

22

The strongest protection is assignment design, not detection. Allow AI openly where it helps, and ask students to submit their prompts and say what they changed. Use the rubric to reward reasoning, evidence and local insight; generic prose earns little. The full red/amber/green statement is in the pack, ready to paste into a course outline, ending with: “A Turnitin AI score alone is never treated as proof.”
