Go to copilotstudio.microsoft.com and sign in with the IBA account. Name format: “[Course code] Study Coach”. Call each checkpoint aloud; green card = done, red card = stuck, helpers go to red cards first. Fast finishers: add a second knowledge file, or start Template 6 (role-play).
