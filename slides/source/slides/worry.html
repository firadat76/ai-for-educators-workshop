The worryThe design rule

AI for Educators: Future-Ready Teaching · IBA-CICT and SMCS

5

0:10–0:20. Two minutes in pairs: “Tell your neighbour one thing you worry AI is doing to your students’ learning, and one boring task you’d happily hand to AI.” Collect three worries aloud and turn each into a design rule on the whiteboard; the rows here are examples to prompt the room. Keep the whiteboard list up all day. Bridge: “Those rules you just wrote are the instructions we’ll give the AI in an hour.”
