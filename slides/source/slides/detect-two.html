3:35–3:42. “Most of us have used the similarity score for years. The AI indicator is a separate number, with separate highlights, and it answers a different question. Similarity asks: does this text match a source? The AI indicator asks: does this prose look like AI writing? Turnitin’s own guide says the AI score should not be used as the sole basis for adverse action against a student.” Bypasser detection (text run through AI humaniser tools) was added in September 2025, English only, and needs the Turnitin Originality / AI add-on licence.
